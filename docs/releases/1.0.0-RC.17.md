# Next UI 1.0.0-RC.17 — preparation

At this preparation checkpoint RC17 **is not yet published**. [RC16](https://github.com/QRun-IO/qqq-frontend-next/releases/tag/v1.0.0-RC.16) remains the latest verified public candidate and is immutable. Planned artifacts are `com.kingsrook.qqq:qqq-frontend-next:1.0.0-RC.17` and `ghcr.io/qrun-io/qqq-frontend-next:1.0.0-RC.17`. Public hashes, signatures and image digests will be recorded after delivery verification. Final 1.0 remains held for application soak and explicit owner approval.

## Source

Main at `6ed2575` (merge of [PR89](https://github.com/QRun-IO/qqq-frontend-next/pull/89)). Merged since RC16: [PR89](https://github.com/QRun-IO/qqq-frontend-next/pull/89) (notification center).

## Backend requirements

Unchanged from RC16 (QQQ **4.1.0-RC.5** or later for the record picker, validation limits and date presets). The notification center renders only when the instance metadata includes the `alerts` app, and needs [qbit-alerts](https://github.com/QRun-IO/qbit-alerts) **0.1.0-RC.2** or later (user notifications); without it nothing renders and no requests are sent. RC17 does not publish QQQ 4.1.

## Features

- **Notification center** ([PR89](https://github.com/QRun-IO/qqq-frontend-next/pull/89)): a bell inbox and notification list with read state and preferences for qbit-alerts user notifications. The center loads on demand (lazy chunk `notificationCenter`, 15.2 KB gzip).

## Bundle budget

**Both the total JavaScript and the first-load budgets are within 2 KB of their caps:** total JS 1098.0 / 1100 KB and `/app` first load 349.2 / 350 KB. The next Next feature needs a measured cap raise (under the owner decision of 2026-10-12) or a code-splitting pass first.
